Program: A sequence of instructions to carry out a specific task.

Program to make tea:
	1. In a sauce pane add milk and water.
	2. Ignite gas stove
	3. Add tea leave once milk and water comes to boil.
	4. Add ginger, sugar to taste.
	5. Boil for some time.
	6. Serve hot.

Algorithm: A step by step procedure to solve a problem.

Algorithm to add two integers:
	1. Accept the first number.
	2. Accept the second number.
	3. Find the sum
	4. Tell the sum
